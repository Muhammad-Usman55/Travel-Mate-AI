Problem Statement

Planning travel involves searching across multiple fragmented platforms for flights, hotels, and itineraries — a time-consuming process. Existing systems lack conversational AI, real-time orchestration, and fault isolation. TravelMate AI solves this with an MCP-based decentralized multi-agent architecture.
